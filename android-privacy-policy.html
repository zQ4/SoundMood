Privacy Policy

SoundMood · Effective August 24, 2026

All Good Things Always, LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") provides SoundMood, an on-device photo-filter application. This Privacy Policy explains how the Android version of SoundMood handles information.

1. Summary

SoundMood does not require an account. The core photo analysis, speech recognition, mood extraction, and filter rendering are designed to occur on your device. We do not upload your selected photos or mood recordings to our servers, and we do not use them to train models.

2. Information You Choose to Process

  • Photos: You select a photo through the Android system photo picker. SoundMood reads that item to preview, classify its scene and subjects, protect relevant regions such as skin tones, render filters, and export a new image. The original is not overwritten.
  • Voice descriptions: If you use the optional microphone feature, SoundMood records up to 15 seconds and processes the recording locally to identify supported mood terms. Temporary audio is deleted after processing or when temporary files are cleared.
  • Preferences: Theme, language, editing choices, permission status, and similar settings may be stored locally on your device.
  • Exports: A processed copy is written only when you ask to save it. SoundMood is designed to remove GPS location metadata from the exported copy while preserving necessary orientation and color information.

3. Permissions

SoundMood requests access only when needed: photo access to select an image, media write access where required by the Android version to save an export, and microphone access when you start a voice description. Internet access is used to load these hosted legal documents inside the app and may support separately disclosed services in a future version. SoundMood does not request location or camera permission.

4. Android Advertising ID (AD_ID)

The current version does not request, access, collect, transmit, or use the Google Advertising ID (AAID/AD_ID), and it does not include an analytics or advertising SDK that uses this identifier. If a future version introduces analytics that uses AD_ID, we will update this Policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosures before collection, request any permission or consent required by law and Google Play policy, identify the analytics provider and purposes, and honor applicable reset, deletion, and opt-out controls. Core photo editing will not require personalized advertising or cross-app tracking.

5. Network and Third-Party Services

The in-app legal viewer retrieves this page from GitHub Pages. GitHub and network providers may receive routine technical data such as IP address, browser or WebView information, timestamps, and request logs under their own policies. If you use Android system services or the system share sheet, information you choose to send is handled by the selected destination under its terms. We do not control those independent services.

6. Collection, Sharing, and Sale

In the current version, we do not operate a backend that collects your photos, recordings, account details, precise location, contacts, or device identifiers. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not show third-party ads. We disclose information only if required by applicable law or necessary to protect rights and safety; because core content remains on-device, we generally do not possess that content.

7. Retention and Deletion

Selected photos remain where your device stores them. Temporary previews, model outputs, and recordings are retained only as needed for processing and are removed through normal cleanup, the in-app “Clear Temporary Files” control, or app removal. Saved exports remain in your photo library until you delete them. Because we do not receive core content on a server, we cannot remotely retrieve or delete it.

8. Security

We use platform safeguards and local processing to limit exposure. No method of storage or software is perfectly secure. Keep your device and operating system updated and use device access controls.

9. Children

SoundMood is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can evaluate the request.

10. Your Choices and Rights

You may deny optional permissions, select different photos, avoid voice input, clear temporary files, delete saved exports, reset local app data, or uninstall SoundMood. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. We will not discriminate against you for doing so. Since current core content is not sent to us, most controls are exercised directly on your device.

11. International Users

Legal-page hosting may process request data in countries other than yours. Applicable protections vary by jurisdiction. Your photos and recordings used by core editing remain on your device unless you independently choose to share them.

12. Changes

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in the app, law, or services. We will revise the effective date and provide additional notice where required. Material new data practices will not be applied retroactively without an appropriate legal basis.
